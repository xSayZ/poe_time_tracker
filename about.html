About Path of Exile Time Tracker

Why this tool was built and how it helps you analyze your campaign runs.

Why I Built This

Look, I am honestly not the fastest Path of Exile player out there. When I run the campaign, I get distracted, spend way too long sorting gear in town, and completely lose track of where my time goes.

Trying to set up complex desktop split timers or wrestling with macro software just to see how badly I was lagging behind always felt like too much friction. I just wanted a simple, frictionless way to look at my Client.txt log and see the cold, hard numbers: where am I actually wasting all my time?

So, I built this tool for myself. And if you're trying to clean up your own campaign and shave off at least a few minutes, hopefully, it can help you too.

Why You Should Use It

  • 100% Private & Local: Your log file is parsed directly inside your browser. Nothing is ever uploaded to a server or stored remotely.
  • Zero Hotkeys Required: You don’t need to remember to hit split keys while dodging boss mechanics or clearing packs. Your act-by-act progression is tracked automatically from your game logs.
  • Find Your Real Bottlenecks: It filters out system noise, chat, and deaths to show pure zone-transition deltas, helping you pinpoint long town stops and AFK gaps instantly.
