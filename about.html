About Path of Exile Time Tracker

Why this tool was built and how it helps you analyze your campaign runs.

Why It Was Built

Tracking campaign split times in Path of Exile usually requires either manual timer software like Livesplit or running third-party desktop tools. This tool was built to give players a lightweight, browser-based alternative that requires zero installation, no dependencies, and no account setup.

Why You Should Use It

  • 100% Local & Private: Your Client.txt log file is parsed directly inside your browser. Nothing is ever uploaded to a server or stored remotely.
  • Automated Act Splits: Instantly breaks down your progression from Act 1 through Act 10 and Endgame Maps without needing to hit hotkeys while playing.
  • Session Breakdowns: Filters out chat, deaths, and system text to show pure zone transition deltas, helping you pinpoint long stops and AFK gaps.
